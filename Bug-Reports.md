# Sample Bug Reports

## Bug 1 – Login Button Does Not Respond

**Bug ID:** BUG-001

**Title:** Login button does not respond after valid credentials are entered

**Environment:**
- Browser: Google Chrome
- Operating System: Windows 11
- Application: Web

**Severity:** High

**Priority:** High

**Precondition:**
User has an active account.

**Steps to Reproduce:**

1. Navigate to the login page.
2. Enter a valid email address.
3. Enter the correct password.
4. Click the Login button.

**Expected Result:**

The user should successfully log in and be redirected to the dashboard.

**Actual Result:**

Nothing happens after the Login button is clicked.

**Status:** Open


## Bug 2 – Shopping Cart Total Is Incorrect

**Bug ID:** BUG-002

**Title:** Shopping cart total does not update after product quantity is changed

**Environment:**
- Browser: Google Chrome
- Application: Web

**Severity:** High

**Priority:** Medium

**Steps to Reproduce:**

1. Add a product to the shopping cart.
2. Open the shopping cart.
3. Change the quantity from 1 to 2.
4. Review the total price.

**Expected Result:**

The total price should update based on the new quantity.

**Actual Result:**

The quantity changes to 2, but the total price still displays the price for one item.

**Status:** Open


## Bug 3 – Search Results Display Unrelated Products

**Bug ID:** BUG-003

**Title:** Product search displays items unrelated to the entered search term

**Severity:** Medium

**Priority:** Medium

**Steps to Reproduce:**

1. Navigate to the homepage.
2. Enter "Laptop" in the search field.
3. Click Search.
4. Review the search results.

**Expected Result:**

Only laptops and closely related products should appear.

**Actual Result:**

Unrelated products such as clothing and household items are displayed.

**Status:** Open
